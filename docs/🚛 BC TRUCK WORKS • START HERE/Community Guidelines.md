# BC TRUCK WORKS Community Guidelines

## 1. Respect
Treat members, drivers, fleet owners, staff, and guests with respect.

## 2. Discord Conduct
No spam, harassment, impersonation, disruptive behavior, or unauthorized advertising.

## 3. Trucking & Roleplay
Drive responsibly and respect convoy, fleet, and roleplay rules.

## 4. Cheating & Exploits
Do not falsify telemetry, manipulate mileage, exploit bugs, or abuse platform systems.

## 5. Account Security
Never share passwords, tokens, API keys, or private configuration information.

## 6. Support & Reports
Use the correct support area and provide useful details. Do not submit knowingly false reports.

## 7. Staff
Follow reasonable staff instructions and use the proper appeal/report process for concerns.

## 8. Enforcement
Violations may result in warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, fleet restrictions, or platform restrictions.

> Guidelines may be updated as BC TRUCK WORKS grows.
